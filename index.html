        About the observatory

        How to read AIETHOS

        AIETHOS is the AI Ethics Observatory, built and curated by Prof Andy Miah. It follows how countries govern artificial intelligence and shows the evidence behind every claim.

        What the scores mean

        Each country is scored from 0 to 100 on eight dimensions: safety, privacy, human oversight, transparency, accountability, fairness, environment and access to remedy. The scores are editorial judgements of protections in force, not forecasts and not official ratings.

        What “provisional” means

        A provisional entry was drafted from secondary sources and has not yet been checked against a primary document such as the law itself. Treat its scores as a starting point.

        How entries are checked

        Every claim links to its sources, tagged primary or secondary, with a confidence level. A watcher checks the library of official documents daily and flags changes. Each entry shows the year it was captured so you can judge how current it is.

        Add a note from your country

        People who study or work on AI in their area can add notes to a country page. Contributors sign in with LinkedIn and are checked once. Every note is read by a moderator before it appears. Open any country and scroll to Community notes.
