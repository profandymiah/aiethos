 Details

 The AI Opportunities Action Plan, led by Matt Clifford CBE , tech entrepreneur and Chair of the Advanced Research and Invention Agency ( ARIA ).

 This document has 50 recommendations for government to:

 grow the UK’s AI sector

 drive adoption of AI across the economy to boost growth

 improve products and services

 Read the government response to the AI Opportunities Action Plan .

 The AI Opportunities Action Plan is a roadmap for government to capture the opportunities of AI to enhance growth and productivity and create tangible benefits for UK citizens. Read the terms of reference here .
