 Global Dialogue on AI Governance

 3–4 May 2027 | New York 
 As artificial intelligence advances rapidly, international cooperation on its governance is more important than ever. The Global Dialogue on AI Governance brings governments and stakeholders from every region together for inclusive discussions informed by independent science.

 About the Dialogue

 Established by General Assembly resolution 79/325, adopted by consensus on 26 August 2025, the Dialogue builds directly on the Global Digital Compact. It is the first global platform of its kind dedicated to AI governance and is open to all Member States.
 Each year, it brings governments together with the private sector, civil society, academia and the technical community to exchange best practices, foster common approaches and help ensure AI governance reflects the priorities of all countries.
 The 2027 Dialogue will take place back-to-back with the 12th Multi-stakeholder Forum on Science, Technology and Innovation for the Sustainable Development Goals . 

 Independent science informing the Dialogue

 The Independent International Scientific Panel on AI provides evidence-based assessments of AI's opportunities, risks and impacts. The Panel's next annual report will inform the 2027 Dialogue.

 2026 Dialogue: Geneva

 The inaugural Global Dialogue on AI Governance was held in Geneva on 6 and 7 July 2026. Delegations from 163 countries took part, alongside representatives of the private sector, civil society, academia and the technical community. The 2026 Dialogue was co-chaired by H.E. Ms. Egriselda López, Permanent Representative of El Salvador, and H.E. Mr. Rein Tammsaar, Permanent Representative of Estonia.
