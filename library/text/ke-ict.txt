 Welcome: Ministry of Information, Communictaion and The Digital Economy 

 Ministry of Information, Communications and The Digital Economy (MICDE) has responsibility for formulating, administering, managing and developing the Information, Broadcasting and Communication policy. In October, 2022, through an Executive Order No. 1/2022, the Ministry was split into two state Departments 

 Learn more about us 

 Vision 

 An informed and Digitally-empowered Kenyan Society

 Mission 

 To facilitate sustainable social economic transformation of Kenya by leveraging on ICT, universal access to Information and Communication services for global communication services for global competitiveness.
