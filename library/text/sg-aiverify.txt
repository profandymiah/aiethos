 What Our New Members Say

 AI providers cannot credibly audit and defend their own systems, the same conflict of interest that made independent financial audit mandatory. Vigil builds an independent accountability and defence layer that records what AI actually does in a tamper-evident format that any party can verify. We are joining AI Verify Foundation because trustworthy AI depends on independent verification and defence, and Singapore is setting the standard the rest of the region will reference.
 Vigil

 At LinkedIn, we are committed to harnessing AI to create economic opportunities for every member of the global workforce. In Singapore, and across the world, we empower professionals to build connections, discover career opportunities, and develop skills, while helping businesses find talent, grow and become more productive. AI is a tool to further our vision, empowering our members and augmenting their success and productivity. We are committed to using AI responsibly and upholding trust in how these technologies are developed and used.
 LinkedIn

 AI agents don&#8217;t fail alone — risk flows in through the tools, MCP servers and skills they trust, so AgentSure independently attack-tests the whole agent and its extension supply chain, producing evidence rigorous enough to underwrite. We join the AI Verify Foundation to bring this testing lens, born from Singapore&#8217;s AI-security research, to the assurance ecosystem and help make trustworthy agentic AI the norm.

 AgentSure Pte. Ltd.

 At Cohere, responsible AI is foundational to building technology that organizations can trust and deploy with confidence. Joining the AI Verify Foundation reflects our commitment to advancing secure, sovereign, and trustworthy AI through rigorous testing and strong governance. We’re proud to support the development of shared, open frameworks that help enterprises and institutions worldwide adopt AI that is reliable, safe, and fully under their control.
 Cohere

 At RajISG, we are building Public Trust Infrastructure for the AI era. Independent testing and verifiable assurance help users engage with AI-enabled services with greater confidence, and give providers a credible way to show their systems have been properly tested. The AI Verify Foundation is making that trust verifiable. We are pleased to join the effort.
 RajISG
